# Aesthetic (mood, not a CAD drawing)

Do **not** implement this file in B0/B1. It exists so later bursts do not drift into a Bambino clone or a skeleton watch.

## Stance

Closed dress watch. The face is the product. The train is a caseback story and a later explode.

Not a copy of Orient Bambino V2 (owner already has one). Steal *grammar* only: dress proportions, a little dome, leather, quiet surface.

## Locked-enough choices

| Item | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Crystal | Sapphire with a **modest dome** (small sag, not a fishbowl) | Better than mineral; keeps dress light; flat sapphire would look more sport-modern than we want |
| Dial | Warm cream / ivory, soft sunburst or very fine circular grain | Pairs with blued hands; not stark white, not navy |
| Hands | **Soft heat-blued steel** — deep indigo-blue, not electric cyan, not teal | Soft hint only |
| Indices | Applied, simple (baton or slim dauphine markers). No Roman chapter, no double railroad | Avoids cloning V2 |
| Date | None on v1 | V2 already has date-at-3 energy |
| Seconds | Small seconds at 6 (fourth arbor after 180°) | Different from typical V2 central seconds |
| Case | Steel, ~40 mm class, polished bezel + mixed finish. Thickness is a stack-up, not “9.8 mm” | |
| Strap | Dark leather for the first visual | |
| Rose gold | **One small accent**, not a two-tone case | See below |

## Rose gold (small on purpose)

Allowed later, one of:

- a thin rose-gold **rehaut or seconds-subdial ring**, or
- rose-gold **crown neck ring / signed cap**, or
- rose-gold **chatons** on the exhibition back only

Do not rose-gold the whole case, lugs, or all indices.
The vendor barrel is already warm gold — that is enough warmth inside the movement.

In Three.js this is just a `MeshPhysicalMaterial` with a rose-gold color (think `0xb76e79`–`0xc9a07a` range), high metalness, moderate roughness. Curiosity is the point; do not let it become the brand.

## Blue, specifically

Heat-blued: dark, a little purple in glancing light. Cream makes it readable. If the cream goes too yellow, cool the dial slightly rather than brightening the hands.

## What “quality” means here

Proportion, dome, restrained dial, finishing on **new** plates later — not more visible gears.
